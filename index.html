About KY Furniture

We make sofas the way your family actually sits.

KY Furniture is a sofa and furniture manufacturer based in Ahmedabad. We design and build sofa sets, recliners, beds and furniture for the whole home in our own unit at Narol – so the frame, the foam, the fabric and the final stitch all pass through our hands before anything reaches your door.

Since we make it ourselves, the choices are yours. Pick the size that fits your wall, a fabric that can handle kids and chai spills, a colour that sits well with your curtains. We build it, deliver it and stand behind it. Modern, stylish and comfortable – apke ghar ka comfort aur style, hamari guarantee.

FAQs

Questions we get asked a lot

Can I order a sofa in my own size and fabric?

Yes. We make every piece ourselves, so you can choose the size, shape, fabric and colour. Share your room measurements or a photo and we will guide you.

How long does a custom order take?

It depends on the design and fabric. We confirm the delivery time when you place the order, so you know the date before you pay anything extra.

Do you deliver and set up the furniture?

Yes. Our own team delivers in Ahmedabad, carries the furniture in, places it in your room and takes the packing away.

Can you repair or re-upholster my old sofa?

Yes. We can change the foam, fabric and stitching so an old sofa looks and feels new again. Send us a photo for a quick estimate on WhatsApp.
